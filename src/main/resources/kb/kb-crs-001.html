Symptom

The learner sees This course is not available for you with SD-CRS-001, while colleagues can open the course.

How to check

  1. The request trail names the exact reason (status, group or enrollment).
  2. Run the diagnostic Why can't a learner see a course? with the learner's e-mail and the course code. The column verdict gives the answer.

Solution

  • course not published: the customer publishes the course.
  • group mismatch: the course was made for another group. Ask the customer whether the learner should get it anyway; then the course's target group or the learner's group must change.
  • not enrolled: the training coordinator enrolls the learner.
